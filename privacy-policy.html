Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how The Unbound Mystic, a Dallas, Texas-based business, handles personal information collected through this website and through direct inquiry communications. It applies to visitors and customers in Texas and throughout the United States, subject to applicable law.

Information Collected

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, selected service or services, reading focus, and optional timing or context. The form opens your email application; the information is sent by you through your email provider rather than stored by the website form itself. We may also receive the information you voluntarily include in emails, messages, booking confirmations, payment communications, customer-service requests, reviews, or other direct communications.

Our website may receive limited technical information through hosting, security, and font-delivery providers, such as IP address, browser type, device information, date and time of access, and request logs. This site does not currently offer user accounts, behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or a public customer profile feature. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How We Use Information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, discuss or confirm requested services, provide readings and customer support, process or reconcile payments where applicable, maintain business and tax records, protect against fraud or misuse, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our terms. We do not use the personal information in your reading request to make automated decisions about credit, housing, employment, insurance, health care, education, or access to essential services.

Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that support our business, such as website hosting, email, payment, bookkeeping, security, or communications providers, but only as reasonably necessary for the services they provide or as required by law. Those providers may process information under their own contractual and privacy obligations. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights, safety, or property, investigate fraud or abuse, or in connection with a business transfer.

Retention and Security

We retain inquiry correspondence, service records, and related business information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain appropriate financial or tax records, comply with legal obligations, and enforce agreements. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No method of email transmission, internet communication, or storage is completely secure; please do not send passwords, Social Security numbers, payment-card details, health records, or other highly sensitive information in an inquiry email.

Texas Privacy Rights

If the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act applies to our processing, Texas residents may request confirmation of processing, access to personal data, correction of inaccuracies, deletion of personal data, and a portable copy of certain data. They may also opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or qualifying profiling. The Unbound Mystic does not sell personal data, use targeted advertising, or conduct qualifying profiling through this website.

To submit a privacy request, email hello@theunboundmystic.com with the subject line “Privacy Request” and enough information to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the request. If a request is denied, you may appeal by emailing the same address with the subject line “Privacy Appeal.” Where the Texas law applies, we will respond within the legally required timeframe and provide information about further complaint options if an appeal is denied.

California Privacy Rights

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, when the law applies to the business. Those rights may include access, deletion, correction, portability, opt-out, limitation, and non-discrimination rights. This site does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not use sensitive personal information beyond providing requested services. California residents may submit a request using the Privacy Request method above. We will honor applicable rights and will not discriminate against a consumer for exercising them.

Children and Minors

This website and its services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us promptly at hello@theunboundmystic.com so we can take appropriate action. If services are ever offered to minors, additional consent and privacy requirements may apply.

External Links, Changes, and Contact

Third-party websites and platforms are governed by their own privacy policies. This policy may be updated as our practices or legal obligations change; the “Last updated” date reflects the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@theunboundmystic.com.
